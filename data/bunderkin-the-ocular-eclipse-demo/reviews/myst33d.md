If I had a dime every time I played an Unreal Engine game, I would have a lot of dimes - shit covered dimes. The engine is ass, it should've died way back in 2006. I don't blame the game developers for this, I blame Epic Games for this, only they know how to use this unholy abomination.

What I'm trying to say is - this game (actually, any Unreal Engine game) requires a lot of resources, I can't run it at high settings at 60 FPS on my RX 6600.

Anyways, the game itself is good. I enjoyed the story and gameplay. Sure, it takes heavy inspiration from The Legend of Zelda, but I don't mind it. I look forward to the full release.

I might've also gotten out of bounds and skipped some story because of this... oops.
