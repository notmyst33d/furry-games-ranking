I got a better PC, but the game didn't get better, I only got to see the worse parts of the game.

I played "TFA ver 2.0.3.rar" from itch.io since other versions either are the same or don't work.

First of all, you can't pause the game, when you press Escape the game keeps running, and the pause menu behaves at random, sometimes you can
press buttons, sometimes you can't. The game also automatically selects "Confirm" option which kicks you out of the game, it's like the game doesn't want you to play.

Turns out the game has RPG-like fighting mechanics, except you don't have to fight, you can flee without any consequence and just run around the enemy. But if you choose to fight, you have to time your clicks to the characters attacks, with the "Twin" attack being the most awful one. There is no indication of when to click.

There is also tons of floating lamps, pipes and other shit. Instead of a skybox you just got a box - no seriously, A DARK BOX.

Visuals are sickening, camera is spazzing all over the place and often getting stuck up the characters ass, the objects are often textureless and use 50 ugly shades of brown. There are electric hurricanes which seem to do nothing and some kind of wireframe glowing orbs.

There is no music besides the battle music and main menu music, am I playing a horror game or an RPG?

Janky movement, janky mechanics, janky enemy models. The entire game is jank, or should I say junk.

Not to mention the Kickstarter campaign, they raised over $10k, yet the game is abandoned in this state.
